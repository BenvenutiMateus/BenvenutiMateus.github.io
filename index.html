About me

I am Mateus Jardim Benvenuti, an undergraduate student in Statistics and Data Science at the Federal University of São Carlos (UFSCar).

I am a member of PET Statistics and hold a CNPq undergraduate research scholarship to study genotype–phenotype graph learning, supervised by Prof. Thiago Rodrigo Ramos .

My interests include statistical modeling, machine learning, and programming. I mainly work with Python and data analysis in my projects.

More information about the project is available on the research page.
