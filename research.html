Research

Genotype–Phenotype Graph Learning Using Random Forests and Stability Selection

Undergraduate research · CNPq scholarship recipient

My undergraduate research investigates statistical and machine learning methods for identifying associations between genotypic and phenotypic variables and representing them as graphs.

The project uses Random Forests to estimate variable importance and stability selection methods to identify robust relationships in high-dimensional data. Based on these relationships, we aim to construct graphs that help us understand the dependence structure between genetic traits and phenotypes.

Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Thiago Rodrigo Ramos , UFSCar.

Research topics

  • Random Forests and variable importance
  • High-dimensional statistics
  • Stability selection
  • Graph learning
  • Statistical modeling of genomic data
